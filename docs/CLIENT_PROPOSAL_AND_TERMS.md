# Client proposal and terms template

Use this when a prospect says they are interested or asks what is included. It is not legal advice; anything unusual goes to a human before the agent promises it.

## Short proposal

Subject: Your website preview for {business_name}

Hi {first_name},

I built the preview so you can judge the work before paying anything:

{preview_url}

If you want it, the pre-built draft price is {currency_symbol}{quoted_price}. That includes:

- The one-page website shown in the preview.
- Mobile-friendly layout.
- Your business name, town, phone/address and call-to-action.
- One round of edits before handover.
- Your photos and logo swapped in if you send them.
- Domain connection or project handover after payment.

Payment link:

{checkout_url}

Once payment clears, I will either transfer the GitHub/Vercel project to you or help connect your domain, depending on what you prefer.

Thanks,
{sender_name}

## Scope

The default sale is one completed one-page website. It is built from the existing preview template and customised with real business facts.

Included:

- One-page responsive website.
- Basic copy adjustments.
- One edit round before handover.
- Image/logo replacement when the client supplies usable files.
- Contact CTA and basic local trust signals already present in the source data.
- Stripe card payment.
- Repo/Vercel handover where applicable.

Excluded unless a human agrees it:

- Ongoing hosting or support plan.
- Multi-page rebuild.
- SEO campaign.
- Paid ads.
- Booking/payment integrations.
- New logo design.
- Copywriting beyond light edits.
- Unlimited revisions.
- Refund guarantees beyond the refund rules below.

## Payment terms

- Payment is due before handover.
- Stripe Checkout is the only automated payment path.
- The checkout amount must match the code-clamped quoted price.
- If the client wants an invoice, VAT/tax wording, bank transfer, split payments, or a subscription plan, escalate to AGENCY-NEGOTIATOR/human.

## Refund and dispute handling

Agents may say:

"If something is wrong with the delivered site, reply here and I will get it looked at. I do not want you stuck with a broken handover."

Agents must not promise:

- A guaranteed ranking increase.
- Guaranteed new customers.
- Unlimited edits.
- A blanket money-back guarantee.
- Legal/compliance review.

Escalate immediately when the client mentions:

- Refund.
- Chargeback.
- Complaint.
- Misrepresentation.
- Data protection.
- Legal threat.
- Accessibility/regulatory requirement.

## Acceptance checklist before sending a proposal

- Lead status is `replied`, `negotiating`, or `payment_sent`.
- Preview URL is public and not behind an auth wall.
- Business facts in the preview are true or safely generic.
- Price is inside `NEGOTIATION_FLOOR` and `NEGOTIATION_CEILING`.
- Checkout URL belongs to this lead and agreed price.
- No refund, legal, accessibility, VAT, tax, or subscription request is being handled automatically.
